A destination wedding is more than an event. It is a complete hospitality experience shaped around a place, its character and the people who come together to celebrate.

The AstraLuxe Advisory helps hospitality destinations and property owners plan for this market by shaping the venue proposition and coordinating the hospitality requirements that contribute to a seamless celebration.

Our approach connects the event experience with the commercial potential of the destination, helping owners consider how wedding-led demand can complement the property's broader hospitality positioning.
