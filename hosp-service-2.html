The Right Agreement. A Stronger Asset.

Hospitality projects can be structured through different operating and brand association models. Each arrangement carries its own commercial structure, responsibilities and implications for the asset.

The AstraLuxe Advisory helps owners understand and evaluate management, revenue-share and franchise arrangements. We assess proposed terms in the context of the project's objectives, operating requirements and long-term direction.

From evaluating commercial considerations to supporting discussions with brands and operators, our role is to bring greater clarity to partnership decisions before owners move towards an agreement.
