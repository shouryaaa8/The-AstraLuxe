The choice of a hospitality brand extends far beyond its name. It influences a property's identity, guest experience, operational philosophy, market positioning and long-term commercial direction.

The AstraLuxe Advisory works alongside hotel owners, landowners and developers to identify suitable domestic and international hotel brands and operating partners. Our evaluation considers the project's location, development scale, investment outlook, intended guest profile and long-term vision.

From initial brand identification and compatibility assessment to partner shortlisting and commercial discussions, we facilitate a structured process that helps clients make informed partnership decisions.
